    Finding species in deep-sea footage

    Annotating biodiversity in deep-sea video used to take more than four months by hand. I designed and evaluated a multi-stage pipeline that detects, tracks, and clusters marine species automatically, bringing that down to 1–2 days.

    • Fine-tuned BioCLIP-2 across 36 configurations with a custom supervised contrastive loss for fine-grained species differences, and built eval metrics that took cluster integrity from about 60% to 96%.
    • Built preprocessing (median blur, CLAHE, background gating) and paired YOLO-World with ByteTrack tracking, detecting individual animals without pre-labeled data at 100% recall on validation footage.
    • Promoted this fall to technical project manager to lead the project's second phase: overseeing a new supervised learning layer, and guiding National Geographic's team as they integrate the tool and build a data pipeline for deep-sea exploration footage.

    Are persona vectors language-invariant?

    Anthropic's persona vectors research found directions in a model's activations that correspond to character traits. I asked whether those directions are the same across languages.

    • Extracted vectors for three traits (evil, sycophancy, hallucination) in English and Spanish on Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and found 0.84–0.92 cross-language cosine similarity, against a 0.45 ceiling between different traits.
    • Validated with an LLM-judged steering evaluation (200 responses per condition). Vectors from the other language suppressed traits as well as same-language vectors did, cutting hallucination by up to 49% without target-language extraction data.
    • Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct
    • Activation steering
    • nnsight
    • LLM-as-judge eval

  • UNICEF Data Science Consultant Sep – Dec 2025

    A RAG chatbot built for young users

    A full-stack chatbot that adapts UNICEF reports for four audiences: children, researchers, policymakers, and humanitarian practitioners. Because some of those users are young or vulnerable, safety was part of the design from the start.

    • Built a 4-stage retrieval pipeline with persona-based prompting for each audience.
    • Identified and documented four recurring failure modes (persona drift, over-complex language, intent injection, and hallucinations in long-context queries) to guide model improvements.
    • Designed guardrails to keep responses age-appropriate, accurate, and non-harmful.
